4. Speech recognition (Apple Speech)

The app uses Apple’s system speech recognition. With “On device only” enabled, recognition runs entirely locally. Without that option, Apple may process audio on its servers. Provider: Apple Distribution International Ltd. / Apple Inc., USA. Apple relies on Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. AI summaries

  • Apple Intelligence (on device): transcript and summary stay entirely on the device. Requires iOS 26 and a supported device.
  • Mistral AI (EU, default cloud provider): the transcript is sent to Mistral AI’s EU endpoints (France). As controller, you should enter into a data processing agreement with Mistral.
  • DeepSeek (optional, non-EU only): processing takes place in China. The app shows an explicit warning before use. Not suitable for personal data in the EU.
  • Custom endpoint: you may configure any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Its operator is then responsible for privacy and contractual terms.

In all cloud cases only text is transmitted, never audio. No transcript is sent to third parties without your active configuration and start of a summary.

6. Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technical access data (e.g. IP address, timestamp, user agent) in server logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in secure delivery (Art. 6(1)(f)). See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Retention

Content remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. We do not store content on our own servers. Cloud providers apply their own retention periods; please review their privacy policies.

8. Recipients and international transfers

Recipients are only the providers you select (Apple, Mistral AI, optionally DeepSeek or your own endpoint) and GitHub for this website. Transfers to the USA rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Transfers to China occur only after your explicit selection and warning.
